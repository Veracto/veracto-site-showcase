# Turning a consulting proposition into audience journeys

## Problem

The site needed to explain practical AI consulting to three audiences without promising that every problem needs AI or that every evaluation necessarily leads to implementation.

## My role

As a Veracto co-founder, Tim Smykov worked on the product structure, interface, implementation and delivery using AI-assisted development. The company site also presents other co-founders, advisors and experts; their roles and source material are not represented as Tim's individual work.

## Decisions

- Keep companies, individual researchers and research teams as distinct routes with shared visual primitives.
- Explain the task and expected work before introducing technical implementation details.
- Separate evaluation, adoption support and training in the offer.
- Use typed content and a static-first implementation rather than add application infrastructure without a concrete need.
- Design phone layouts first, retain semantic navigation and provide reduced-motion behavior.
- Preserve a boundary between intended outputs and observed client outcomes.

## Result

The public edition includes the selected original bilingual site source, reviewed public imagery, responsive layouts and a reproducible static build. The preview is a portfolio copy; existing business deployment and client data are separate.

The outcome demonstrated here is a working public interface. No adoption, conversion, revenue or return-on-investment claim is made without separate evidence.
